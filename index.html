Privacy notice

What we collect. Your name, contact numbers, email and city; journey details; and the name, age, gender and berth preference of each passenger. We do not ask for Aadhaar numbers, IRCTC passwords, OTPs or payment details — please never share these in this form.

How it is used. Only to look for suitable trains, process this booking request and contact you about it.

Where it goes. When you submit, your request is sent over an encrypted connection to the booking desk's private storage. Only booking desk staff can see it — it is never published on this website or its code repository. A CSV copy is also downloaded to your device for your records.
